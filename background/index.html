How I work

Most engagements start the same way. A firm has data scattered across spreadsheets, SaaS tools, document folders, and people's heads. There's no source of truth and no way to ask a question without three people in a meeting. The path forward is roughly:

  1. Audit. Map the data landscape. Most engagements want to skip this and start modeling; that's the expensive mistake. You end up modeling noise and rebuilding six months later.
  2. Centralize. Design and build the ingestion and storage layer — document parsing, normalization, structured storage, and the dimensional modeling that makes everything downstream possible. dbt and Snowflake handle most of this well; the work is in the modeling, not the tooling.
  3. Surface. Add the querying layer. A Looker dashboard, a search interface, a natural-language query layer over the warehouse, or some combination. Pick by who's asking the questions, not by what's fashionable.
  4. Hand off. Documentation, runbooks, and the lightweight maintenance procedures an existing team needs to keep the system running. The goal is a system your team can own — not a dependency on me.

I take on discrete projects and build-then-handoff engagements, working solo or alongside other specialists.
