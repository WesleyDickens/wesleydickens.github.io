How I work

Most engagements start the same way. A firm has data scattered across spreadsheets, SaaS tools, document folders, and people's heads. There's no source of truth and no way to ask a question without three people in a meeting. The path forward is roughly:

  1. Audit. Map the data landscape. Identify what's actually needed, what's noise, and where the real bottlenecks are.
  2. Centralize. Design and build the ingestion and storage layer — document parsing, normalization, structured storage, and the modeling work that makes everything downstream possible.
  3. Surface. Add the querying layer. Depending on the client that's a dashboard, a search interface, a natural-language query system over the data, or some combination.
  4. Hand off. Documentation, runbooks, and the lightweight maintenance procedures an existing team needs to keep the system running. Or stay on retainer if that's a better fit.

I take on discrete projects and build-then-handoff engagements, working solo or alongside other specialists.
