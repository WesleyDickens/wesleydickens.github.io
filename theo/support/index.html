Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the theologian responses?

Responses are AI-generated interpretations grounded in what the theologians actually wrote — not fabricated quotes or invented citations. Every response passes through a verification pipeline that checks claims against real sources before surfacing them. You'll see citations you can follow up on.

That said, these are interpretations, not the theologians' actual words. For authoritative guidance, consult primary sources or a qualified pastor or scholar. See How It Works for the full picture.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. I use OAuth 2.0 authentication and do not sell personal information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
