Theo

Theologian-attributed Q&A with structured citations

Theo is an iOS app for asking theological questions and receiving responses attributed to specific theologians — Augustine, Aquinas, Luther, Calvin, and C.S. Lewis. Interpretation is presented in third person, direct quotes are visually distinct and always cited, and every source is verifiable.

It is currently in closed beta. If you'd like access, get in touch. The motivation behind the project is on the welcome page.
